HOUSE INFO

Lights

The house runs on Leviton Decora Smart Wi-Fi switches and dimmers. Control them at the wall like normal, or from your phone with the My Leviton app.

The My Leviton app

Download My Leviton (free, iOS & Android) to control lights from your phone. You don't need an account to use the switches on the wall — the app is only for remote and scheduled control. Ask the family for a guest invite if you'd like app access during your stay.

At the wall

Tap the top of the paddle for on, the bottom for off. Press and hold to dim up or down. The small LED bar on the switch shows the current brightness level, even when the light is off.

Dimming & scenes

Dimmers remember your last brightness. In the app you can set scenes — like a low "Evening" setting for the living room and balcony — and trigger them with one tap.

Wi-Fi network

The switches are already connected to the house Wi-Fi (paradise7557). Leviton devices use the 2.4 GHz band — no setup needed on your end.

If a light won't respond

Toggle the switch off and on at the wall first — that fixes most hiccups. If it's still stuck, the switch may be offline; it reconnects to Wi-Fi on its own within a minute or two. Please don't factory-reset any switch.
